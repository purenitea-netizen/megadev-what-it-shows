The two problems

  1. parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant whose people write the day first gets a month flip. This is the problem the fix on #4471 covers. A few reports read as a one-day move; the table counts them here and footnotes them.
  2. hint. Tenants on booking-v3 get the hint MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it day first. A different bug, in the form, not the parser. It only shows up in the form channel, and it has no ticket of its own yet.

Intake’s tag comes from the reporter’s browser and is a guess. The table shows how often it disagrees with the cause. Use the tenant’s locale and booking form.
