The three problems

  1. parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. A tenant whose people write the day first gets a month flip. This is the problem the fix on #4471 covers.
  2. hint. Tenants on booking-v3 get the hint MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads it day first. A different bug, in the form, not the parser. It only shows up in the form channel.
  3. one-day. The desk notes list reports of a single day off: “only June”, an invite that arrived before the booking, one meeting that was Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto. A month flip does not do that. It points at time zones. Nobody has found the cause yet.
